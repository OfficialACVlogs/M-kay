1958 · Early games

Brookhaven National Laboratory first showed Tennis for Two to visitors on October 18, 1958. It ran on an analog computer and used an oscilloscope as its display.

Bring your own ROM

Only choose game files you have the right to use. No ROMs are included. EmulatorJS is loaded from its CDN when you start a game, so an internet connection is required.

About the emulator

Playback uses the EmulatorJS N64 core. Game compatibility and speed depend on your browser and device.

Safe wallpapers

Choose from built-in abstract designs. Image uploads and external wallpaper links are disabled, so unreviewed images cannot be selected.

Chromebook-friendly startup

Starts the documented Mupen64Plus Next N64 core with threading off, so it does not need special cross-origin headers. Game speed still depends on the Chromebook and ROM; if it stutters, lower resolution or disable shaders in emulator settings.
